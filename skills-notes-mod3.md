# Module 3: Job Posting Skills Notes

## Posting 1: Data Analyst Intern
**Seniority:** Entry-level internship  
**Industry:** Data analytics

### Skills and Qualifications
**Required:**
- Currently enrolled in a bachelor's or master's degree program in a related field.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Attention to detail and commitment to data accuracy.
- Strong communication and organizational skills.
- Ability to work independently and collaboratively.

**Preferred:**
- Familiarity with SQL, Python, or data analysis tools.
- Experience with Power BI or Tableau.
- Experience with data cleaning, transformation, and validation.
- Experience with cloud platforms such as Databricks, Snowflake, Azure, or AWS.

## Posting 2: Data Science Intern
**Seniority:** Entry-level internship  
**Industry:** Data science

### Skills and Qualifications
**Required:**
- Enrollment in a bachelor's or master's degree program in a listed field.
- Graduation between December 2027 and June 2029.
- Ability to define problems, collect data, establish facts, and draw valid conclusions.
- Experience working with Python, R, and SQL.
- Knowledge of advanced statistical concepts, including random forests, ensemble methods, nearest-neighbor methods, and K-Means.
- Strong written, verbal, and analytical skills.
- Must be at least 18 years old.

**Preferred:**
- No qualifications were labeled as preferred.

## Skills Shared by Both Postings
- Enrollment in a related degree program.
- SQL and Python.
- Analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Experience working with and analyzing data.
- Communication skills.

## Reflection

What stood out to me most was how much the two postings had in common despite being for different roles. Both employers wanted candidates with analytical skills, programming experience, and the ability to work with data. The main difference was that the data science internship emphasized advanced statistical methods, while the data analyst internship focused more on data preparation and business intelligence tools.
